## Summary
In 2025 the company sold 1,866 thousand USD across four regions. Growth came mainly from North and West, which rose in every quarter. East stayed the largest region by a small margin. South was flat and dipped in the middle of the year before recovering in Q4. The overall pattern is healthy, but it depends on two regions for most of the growth, and the board should watch the balance between them closely during the coming year.

## Key figures
- North: 575 thousand USD, up from 120 in Q1 to 170 in Q4.
- South: 374 thousand USD, lowest at 88 in Q3.
- East: 601 thousand USD, the largest region.
- West: 316 thousand USD, up from 60 in Q1 to 99 in Q4.
- Company total: 1,866 thousand USD.

## Risks
South declined for two quarters in a row before Q4, which may signal weaker local demand or stronger competitors. East grew slowly after Q2, so the largest region may be close to saturation. West is still small, so its strong growth adds less in absolute terms than the percentages suggest. Relying on North for most new revenue concentrates risk in one market.

## Recommendations
1. Review the South region's pricing, staffing and competitor activity before the next budget cycle.
2. Keep investing in West, where each quarter beat the previous one, and set a target for 2026.
3. Protect the East region's position with a retention programme for its largest customers.
4. Report regional results quarterly to the board, with a short note on the drivers behind each change.
5. Ask finance to add margin data, so that the next summary can show profit as well as sales.
